Additional Pricing Structures

A detailed overview of standalone, a-la-carte, and custom commercial commissions.

01 / A-La-Carte

Photography Packages

If you only require high-end stills without video or virtual tours, we offer dedicated architectural photography rates.

  • Essential Stills ($350): 12 HDR interior/exterior wide stills, ideal for basic MLS entries.
  • Premium Stills ($550): 25 retouched editorial stills, custom twilight scheduling included.
  • Architectural Stills ($900): 40 high-resolution full-frame detail captures, focus stacking, and polarization.
  • Twilight Add-On ($200): Convert any daytime photography package to include sunset glow sweeps.
02 / Standalone Motion

Video Packages

Engaging, motion-guided real estate walkthrough videos tailored for landing pages, social media, and client portfolios.

  • Social Media Reel ($300): 30-60 second vertical video cut (9:16) optimized for Instagram & TikTok.
  • Cinematic Showcase ($600): 2-minute horizontal walkthrough (16:9) featuring smooth gimbal orbits.
  • Complete Cinematic Suite ($850): 3-minute listing video with custom sound design and licensed music.
  • Aerial Drone Loop ($250): Standalone 4K drone cinematography sweep showing absolute lot boundaries.
03 / Custom Commissions

Custom Projects

For builders, interior designers, architects, and hospitality brands needing tailored media portfolios.

Commercial projects require specific licensing agreements depending on where and how the assets will be distributed. We work on custom daily rates or package quotes.

  • Half-Day Rate ($650): Up to 4 hours on-site, capturing detailed textures and lighting conditions.
  • Full-Day Rate ($1,200): Up to 8 hours on-site, planning scenes around the path of the sun.
  • Asset Licensing: Commercial distribution rights tailored for magazines, advertisements, and websites.
  • Staging & Styling: Collaborative staging guidance to ensure rooms show clear spatial depth.
04 / Package Guidelines

Per-Listing Packages

Our standard flat-rate bundles are governed by simple, transparent scheduling guidelines.

  • Square Footage Rules: Flat rates cover properties up to 4,000 sq ft. Larger properties are quoted individually.
  • Travel Radii: Round-trip travel within 50 miles of the studio is included. Travel beyond is billed at $0.75/mile.
  • Revision Policy: Includes one round of minor color and sequence revisions. Extra edits are billed hourly.
  • Delivery Timelines: Digital assets are delivered within 24-48 hours via secure hosting links.
