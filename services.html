Detailed Service Breakdowns

Explore the custom workflows, advanced techniques, and premium deliverables provided for each media format.

01 / Stills

Real Estate Photography

Professional high-end photography forms the foundation of any luxury listing. Using state-of-the-art camera bodies and wide-angle lenses, I capture interior and exterior spaces with dramatic precision.

Every photo goes through a meticulous hand-blending retouched workflow, correcting lens distortions and balancing interior/exterior color profiles.

What's Included & Workflow

  • Flambient Blending: Combination of flash and ambient exposures for optimal window views and accurate interior color tones.
  • Perspective Control: Correction of vertical and horizontal lines so structural elements remain perfectly parallel.
  • Premium Retouching: Sky replacement (for overcast days), digital cleaning, and object removal.
  • Twilight Shoots: Capturing properties during the golden hour to showcase exterior architectural lighting.

What's Included & Workflow

  • Part 107 Compliance: All flights are conducted legally by an FAA-licensed and fully insured remote pilot.
  • High-Resolution Stills: 48MP aerial photography capturing massive spreads, details, and site plans.
  • Cinematic Fly-Bys: Smooth, slow motion orbits and sweeping reveal movements for marketing videos.
  • Boundary Overlays: Clear digital drawing of property lines and lot borders onto aerial stills.
02 / Aerial

Drone Photography

Showcase a property's surrounding environment, landscape, and absolute scope. Aerial drone media offers essential context for large luxury estates, commercial acreage, and waterfront properties.

Providing legal, certified drone solutions that capture neighborhood locations, coastal boundaries, and stunning bird's-eye sweeps.
03 / Motion

Cinematic Video

Movement tells a lifestyle story. I produce premium, high-definition video walkthroughs that guide prospective buyers through the architectural highlights of a home.

Films are shot in full 4K UHD, featuring slow, deliberate gimbal transitions, licensed soundtrack scoring, and professional color grading.

What's Included & Workflow

  • Gimbal Stabilization: Smooth walk-through motion that simulates a continuous, fluid architectural path.
  • Licensed Music: Soundtracks curated and licensed for commercial real estate marketing across all platforms.
  • Social Media Cuts: 30 to 60-second vertical formats optimized for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.
  • Agent Walkthroughs: Optional microphone setup and teleprompter support for presenting agents on-camera.

What's Included & Workflow

  • Matterport Pro3 LiDAR: High-precision laser scans mapping up to 100 meters, ideal for large residential and commercial properties.
  • Schematic Floor Plans: Professional, dimensioned black-and-white 2D floor plans delivered in PDF format.
  • Interactive Hotspots: Clickable text or media dots embedded in the tour to detail high-end finishes, views, or appliance models.
  • Permanent Hosting: Secure digital hosting links provided for integration directly onto MLS, Zillow, or personal websites.
04 / Immersive

Virtual Tours

Provide continuous access to listings with fully immersive digital twin models. Prospective buyers can walk through rooms virtually at their own pace, inspecting layouts from anywhere in the world.

Perfect for remote high-value investors. LiDAR scans construct detailed 3D dollhouse maps, facilitating virtual spatial orientation.
