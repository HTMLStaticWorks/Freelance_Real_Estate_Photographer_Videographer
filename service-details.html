Cinematic Property Video Production

High-end marketing films designed to tell the architectural story of your luxury listing.

My Cinematic Video Philosophy

Real estate videos are often quick walkthroughs that feel robotic. I take a fundamentally different approach. I treat every property as a cinematic canvas, planning scenes, camera moves, and lighting to emphasize space and material quality.

I coordinate closely with agents to identify the most unique design assets of the home: a custom floating staircase, a zero-edge infinity pool, or an expansive kitchen layout. I capture these details using state-of-the-art stabilizers, 4K camera systems, and drone trajectories that highlight the property's surrounding environment.

Workflow & Deliverables

Each video project goes through a rigorous post-production phase where I apply professional color grading, edit-matching music tracks, and subtle audio designs.

  • Pre-Shoot Coordination: We review the listing's layout, sun angles, and timeline.
  • Raw Capture: Detailed 4K drone orbits + interior gimbal walks.
  • Editing: 60-second social media reel + 3-minute full cinematic property film.
  • Delivery: Shared download link containing custom MLS-ready links within 48 hours.
